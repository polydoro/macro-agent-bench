# Limitations

1. **Small study.** 16 tasks, five of them complex. With 16 tasks, steps of less than about two points along the frontier are within noise.
2. **One run per cell.** Run-to-run variance of the agents (same prompt, new run) is not measured. Judge variance is (see judge-validation.md).
3. **Frontier lines show each method's best models.** A method on the frontier is not good for every model: two of the nine models scored lower with examples.
4. **Review started from the low-effort run.** Review on top of examples, or on top of high effort, was not tested.
5. **High effort mixes effort and information.** Runs happened on different dates (26 October to 7 November 2026), with official data changing between them; a later run could see a newer release. Answer keys are built per method and run date, so each delivery is scored against what was available when it ran.
6. **"Low" and "high" are each CLI's own setting.** They do not equalise compute across labs.
7. **The worked examples** were made over time with earlier AI models and refined. Whether that favours any lab was not tested. Only one of them is published (examples/es-industrial-turnover/).
8. **Judge.** Validated on 15 deliveries only; intervals are wide. P2 (identification) is stricter than my labels. The legibility recheck used readers from a tested lab.
9. **Keys come from the agents' own downloads.** There is no independent download of each vintage; within a publisher stamp all agents' copies had to agree cell by cell, and the keys were cross-checked against press releases where available.
10. **Some C03 nowcasts are not yet scored on accuracy** (Q4 2026 targets); their totals are rescaled from 90 points.
11. **Time, not cost.** Runs used CLI subscriptions, so money cost is unknown, not zero.
12. **My tasks.** Euro-area macro releases and questions, delivered in Portuguese. Other work could give a different frontier.
